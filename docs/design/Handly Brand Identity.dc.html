02 — The Decision

We're building on the Verified H.

Every concept is viable — but only one leads with the exact feeling the brief demands: "I can trust this to fix my home." The Verified H fuses Handly's letter with the checkmark, the most universally understood symbol of safety and approval on earth. It's the most ownable, the fastest to read, and the only one that stays legible at 16 pixels. We refine it into the final identity.
